# OpenPAVE v1.8.1 — General ROS-free body

**Date:** 2026-09-03 · **Tag:** `v1.8.1`

A small release that makes the "**body is a plugin**" claim concrete: two general, **ROS-free**
actuator adapters, a first-timer demo runbook, and the spec positioning behind it. Built on
[`v1.8.0`](v1.8.0.md); purely additive — the seam, the `{action, params}` contract, and the runtime
are unchanged.

## Highlights

- **`gpio_servo` adapter** — a hobby servo over GPIO (`gpiozero`, **no ROS 2**): `stop`/`home` center
  the horn, `trot` sweeps it continuously (background thread; dispatched once, held until `stop`),
  `move` steers by `params.yaw`. Lazy import + dry-run fallback, so it imports and tests anywhere.
- **`led` adapter** — a single PWM LED as a status body (`stop` solid, `trot` pulses, …), for a
  zero-motion, zero-risk indicator. Software-validated.
- Both follow `LocomotionCapabilityMixin` — just another `CapabilityAdapter` behind the seam.
  Registered as `ROBOT_ADAPTER=gpio_servo` / `led`. 20 dry-run unit tests
  (`tests/test_gpio_servo_adapter.py`, `tests/test_led_adapter.py`).
- **[v1.8 Quick Demo](../runbooks/v1.8-quick-demo.md)** — a self-contained runbook: an RPi 5 camera +
  one servo as a general body, from a dry-run pipeline to the live loop, then the one-line swap to
  PuppyPi. Safety card, wiring, bring-up, teardown, troubleshooting.
- **Spec positioning** — [`openpave-platform-spec.md`](../openpave-platform-spec.md) now states the
  **body dimension is protocol-neutral**: adopting a controller is a robot adapter mapping
  `{action, params}` to its native interface; ROS 2 is one body's interface (PuppyPi), lives inside
  that adapter below the seam, is not required, and is not plug-and-play. `pave_runtime` has no ROS 2
  dependency.

## Real-hardware validation (2026-09-03)

The general body was validated end to end: DGX brain `run_live` (mock `TROT`) → `raw_zenoh` → RPi 5
**real `gpio_servo` body** (`AngularServo` on GPIO18). The motion was confirmed on the 2nd frame,
`trot` dispatched, the body returned `completed` with the servo detail `{sweep_deg: 40, hz: 2}` (the
servo swept), edge-triggered hold, then the bounded run **auto-STOPped and centered**. Same seam and
contract as the PuppyPi rows — **only `ROBOT_ADAPTER` changed**. See
[`docs/validation-matrix.md`](../validation-matrix.md).

## Compatibility

Additive. No change to the seam plugin, the runtime, the inference/application boundary, or the
PuppyPi paths from `v1.8.0`. New bodies are new adapters; nothing above the seam changes.
